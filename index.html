// Our Story

12 Years of Healing
Rajkot, One Patient
at a Time

Founded with a clear mission — to put patients first in a world that often puts profit first — Planet Health Care has grown to become one of Rajkot's most trusted physiotherapy clinics. Situated at the heart of Royal Park, Kalavad Road, we have spent over a decade restoring movement, reducing pain, and transforming lives.

Every treatment plan we create is personal. We listen, assess, and design a recovery journey that fits you — not a template. From advanced equipment therapy to hands-on manual techniques, we combine clinical science with genuine human care.
